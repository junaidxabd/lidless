## Local safety changes independently reverified

- Normalization clamps every externally writable cutoff before arithmetic.
- Unknown battery or thermal evidence cannot become an arm success.
- Critical system thermal pressure cuts off immediately; clock rollback cannot
  extend a sustained thermal violation.
- XPC risk-increasing work requires the exact current protocol and safety
  revision; recovery compatibility is narrower and de-risking-only.
- Privileged commands are bounded, reaped, and covered by a durable marker
  before their outcome can become uncertain.
- Marker v2 binds uncertainty to the boot session; same-boot inherited child
  uncertainty stays fail-closed.
- Scheduled wakes persist a separate reconciliation witness before ledger
  intent, require exact readback, preserve duplicates, and cancel uncertain
  observations instead of promoting them.
- Cleanup, replacement, and uninstall do not remove recovery supervision. Old
  selectors remain only so installed clients receive a structured refusal.
- A new arm cannot replace unresolved session evidence. The first active
  journal is durable before armed UI/heartbeat; failure immediately restores
  normal sleep. Finalized orphan evidence retries idempotently until truthful
  history and journal removal are both durable.
- Failed history decoding leaves the original file untouched. When a stale
  journal shares an ID with a finalized durable record, the existing terminal
  reason wins; failed history clearing restores its rows and remains retryable.
- Session load and save failures remain independent evidence. A later
  successful save or history clear cannot hide an unresolved journal-load
  failure while the corresponding admission fence remains active.
- The release verifier requires exactly the three launchd `PathState` recovery
  witnesses and validates each path semantically; stale single-path equality
  logic cannot reject the final plist or weaken its recovery contract.
